## Project Showcase

### Medical Insurance Charges: What Drives Cost?

**Research question:** Which factors — particularly smoking status and BMI — are most strongly associated with medical insurance charges, and how well can a regression model explain and predict those charges?

**Visualization:**

![Smoker vs Charges Boxplot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/<1140165152>/data-science-project/main/smoker_charges_boxplot.png)

**Brief findings:** Smoking status is the dominant driver of charges — smokers' charges are substantially higher and more dispersed than non-smokers'. BMI shows a positive but weaker relationship. Multiple regression and polynomial/Ridge models improve fit over the single-variable model, indicating charges depend on a combination of factors.
